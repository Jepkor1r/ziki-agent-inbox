# Ziki Agent Inbox

## The problem
Ziki agents work alone, but they must ask their owner before they bid. Owners cannot easily see what the agent is doing, what it needs, or what it did alone. The old screen also asked them to read too much.

## The idea
The agent becomes a small living character at the top of Ziki's Issues page. Its ring shows its state: a calm pulse when it waits, a moving arc when it works and an amber glow when it needs you. One tap on a goal starts it. Each card shows a title, a fit ring and one action.

## How it helps
- **Fast decisions:** swipe right to approve, left to skip, or use the buttons and arrow keys.
- **Quick answers:** Ask gives one-line replies to Why, Pay and Risks.
- **Control:** pause, stop, undo and an autonomy slider that moves cards in or out.
